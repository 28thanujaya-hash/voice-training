# Maya mood selection rules (agreed with the mentor 17 Nov 2026, 15:20–16:58)
All numbers are against Maya's normal (median of the clean normal clips). Speed = words only (pauses not counted).

| | Low | Normal | Bright | Tense |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | 15–40% slower | within ±25% (no drag, no rush) | not slower (≥ −5%) | ≥ 10% faster |
| Loudness | ≥ 2 dB quieter | — | — | ≥ 3 dB louder |
| Pitch | not used | — | ≥ +2.5 st | ≥ +2 st |
| Pitch movement | — | not dead flat (≥ −1.0 SD) | ≥ +0.8 SD | — |
| Breathiness (H1-H2) | ≥ +3 dB | — | ≤ +1.5 dB | — |
| End of the line | — | — | end not quieter (≥ −1 dB), pitch fall gentle (≥ −5 st) | end ≥ 3 dB quieter (hard drop), pitch falls through the line |
| Length | short OK | — | ≥ 6 syllables | short OK |

**Normal** = everything that does not lean toward a mood. Leaning = halfway to that mood's limits on its whole bundle:
lowish = ≤ −7.5% speed AND ≤ −1 dB AND ≥ +1.5 dB breathiness; tenseish = ≥ +1 st AND ≥ +5% speed AND ≥ +1.5 dB AND end ≤ −1.5 dB;
brightish = ≥ +1.25 st AND ≥ +0.4 movement AND end ≥ −0.5 dB.

**All moods:** longest gap inside a line ≤ 0.5 s; no flat held note > 0.5 s (pitch within ±0.5 st); no hiss > 0.15 s (>90% of energy above 4 kHz);
start/end silence trimmed (0.1 s kept). A clip only counts for the mood it was recorded as (others dropped). At least half of each mood's clips full sentences.

## Applied to the 1,661 run-5 Orpheus clips (measure rules only; gap/hold/hiss not yet checked)
| mood | clips passing | lines with ≥1 clip |
|---|---|---|
| normal | 214/365 | 116/152 |
| bright | 7/337 | 7/128 |
| low | 9/590 | 7/130 |
| tense | 8/369 | 6/143 |

No clip passes two moods (the rules do not overlap). But Orpheus hits a mood's full bundle in only ~2% of takes →
moods cannot be selected from random takes; recording must aim at the bundle.
